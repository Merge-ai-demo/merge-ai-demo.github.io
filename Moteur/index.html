Web search

Your AI never goes on the internet by itself. When you turn on “Web”, Merge first turns your question into a few search words, sends only those words to the sources you choose below: the Keenable search engine (the whole web), Wikipedia, the GDELT news database (recent headlines), PubMed and OpenAlex (scientific articles). It gives the results to your AI, with their links. Your chat and your files stay on this Mac. Each search is listed in the log above.

My data

Allow web search?

To answer with recent information, Merge searches the web for your AI, then gives it the results to read.

Only a few search words leave this Mac: Merge first turns your question into search words, and sends them to Wikipedia. Your chat and your files stay here.

Like any website, these services see the search words and your internet address. Keenable may use the searches it receives to train and improve its products, and this free service can stop at any time (its terms, keenable.ai). You can turn “Web” off at any time, and withdraw this agreement in Settings › Privacy.
